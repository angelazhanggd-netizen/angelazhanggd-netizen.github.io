Conversion Rate

R-101

Leaching

NCM + H2SO4 + H2O2

The acid-peroxide matrix breaks down the layered metal-oxide lattice of spent NCM cathode powder, releasing Ni, Co, Mn and Li as free ions in solution for near-complete extraction.

Precipitation

Selective Precipitation

[M2+][OH–]2 > Ksp

LiOH precipitant is dosed dropwise to raise OH– concentration in controlled stages; once the ion product exceeds each hydroxide's solubility product Ksp, Ni(OH)2, Co(OH)2 and Mn(OH)2 crystallise out in sequence.

R-103 · S-103/104

Electrolysis & Separation

Li2SO4 + 2H2O → 2LiOH + H2SO4

Residual Li2SO4 is electrolytically split in R-103; the stream then routes to S-103 for LiOH purification and S-104 for H2SO4 recovery, with spent liquor purged to close the loop.

05

Literature Comparison & Engineering Optimization

Literature Baseline

2× RGibbs + 2× Flash2

Equilibrium black-box units with limited auditability of underlying chemistry.

Optimized Model (This Study)

3× RStoic + 3× Sep

Explicit stoichiometric coefficients with component-traceable separation.

01

De-blackboxing & Auditability

Replacing the RGibbs "black box" — which cannot specify explicit stoichiometric coefficients — with explicit RStoic reactions makes every conversion auditable.

Back-calculated from literature Fig. 3 @ 185 kg/h acid flow: residual H2SO4 = 7.56 kg/h, O2 = 2.82 kg/h

True H2SO4:NCM = 1.335 : 1 (not the literal 1.5 : 1) → H2O2 coefficient calibrated to 0.05

Corrects literature's simplified atom balance — 11% H2SO4 deviation, 9× H2O2 deviation

02

Robustness with Incomplete Data

Since K = exp(-ΔG / RT), a small error in an estimated ΔGform for missing components like NCM715 can swing RGibbs product distributions wildly.

K = exp(-ΔG / RT)

Neumann-Kopp fills solid ΔHform: Ni(OH)2 / Mn(OH)2 = -167.3 kcal/mol, Co(OH)2 = -129 kcal/mol

Custom NCM oxide: ΔHform = -140,800 cal/mol, ρ = 4.8 g/cm³ → Vspoly = 20.2 cm³/mol

03

Gas–Solid Separation Restructure

Flash2 defaults to vapor-liquid equilibrium and cannot correctly represent solid precipitates, so it is replaced with explicit, component-traceable Sep blocks.

Sep1 — separates unreacted NCM from gas

Sep2 — separates all Ni/Co/Mn(OH)2 solids

Every component tracked through a rigorous, traceable mass balance

04

Reproducible Parameter Calibration

Rather than copying literature coefficients verbatim, every parameter is back-calculated against measured data — a fully interpretable, reproducible engineering methodology.

H2O2 coefficient 0.05, calibrated from 185 kg/h acid flow

>99% dissolution efficiency retained alongside 7.56 kg/h residual H2SO4
